## 2. THE PIECE

`assets/gear/artifact/sceptre_golden.glb` - 324 verts, 236 tris, 3 primitives,
30 KB, generated by `tools/art/_sceptregen.py` (bmesh, no pack geometry).

Against Mr. E's three rules:
- **PLAINNESS.** A mace you could hit with and a token of office, in that
  order: cast collars, an octagonal tapered shaft, six blunt flanges, a wrapped
  grip. No filigree, nothing gothic.
- **WEAR IS A RECEIPT.** Tier 5, so the metal is bright and new and the stone
  is clean. The brightness is not a taste call - see section 4.
- **IT READS.** The head is RADIALLY SYMMETRIC - six flanges and six crown
  points around the axis. That is the deliberate answer to the wand's fault:
  the wand's crystal read front and back and vanished side-on because its
  silhouette depended on which way the head faced. A mace head has no front, so
  no angle can hide it. **WHAT THAT COSTS, SAID OUT LOUD:** there is no
  front-facing detail to catch the eye, so the stone at the apex carries the
  whole read on its own - which is why section 5 spent a pass on it.

## 3. MY OWN FAULT THIS RUN - I BUILT IT ON THE WRONG AXIS AND MY GATE ENFORCED IT

`gear.gd` says of the golden set: "built along +Y with the grip at the origin".
I took that sentence as the authoring convention, turned the mesh onto +Y, and
then wrote an assertion REQUIRING +Y - so my own instrument certified the
mistake. The comment is true in the glTF/Godot frame and false in the frame
these scripts work in.

**WHAT CAUGHT IT WAS THE NUMBERS DISAGREEING WITH THEMSELVES**, before any
picture: the engine's identity carry and the upright `(0,0,90)` carry came back
reach 0.107 and 0.101, moved 1888 and 1864 - two carries ninety degrees apart
that cannot both look the same. I measured instead of reasoning
(`tools/art/_sceptre_axis_probe.py`): imported into Blender, `sword_golden`
spans z 2.302, `staff_rare` z 1.162, `wand_crystal` z 0.921. **EVERY SHIPPED
WEAPON'S LONG AXIS IS BLENDER +Z**, and the calibrated staff basis sends mesh
+Z to slot -X, which is up. The generator now builds on +Z and both gates
(generator and carry harness) assert +Z with the measurement written beside
them. A NOTE'S WORDING IS NOT A MEASUREMENT.
