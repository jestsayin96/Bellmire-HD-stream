rpg/ (CC0 1.0 - https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
  Procedural RPG SFX generated with Mr. E's own music-studio (jestsayin96/music-studio v3.5.2,
  `npm run sfx`). Original synthesized audio, released CC0. 86 ids x up to 2 variants: voices
  (male/female effort, hurt, death, laugh...), monsters (orc, troll, dragon, goblin, demon,
  skeleton, zombie, ghost, wraith, slime...), animals (wolf, bear, lion, horse, dog, cat, birds,
  insects...), magic (fire, ice, lightning, heal, buff, curse, shield, summon, teleport...),
  combat (sword, dagger, bow, shield, footsteps...) and ui (level_up, chest, coins, potion,
  menu...). The music-studio repo is left untouched; these files were copied in.
