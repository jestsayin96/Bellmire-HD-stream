# DOES A GOLD MESH READ AS "ARTIFACT" WHEN ARTIFACT IS SCAR-RED?

Asked of the ART lane by the team lead, 18 Nov 18:3x CT, as an opinion wanted
BEFORE the game build's System 6 conversion of the seven Ultimate-RPG `_Golden`
FBX - not a decision anybody has to escalate. **Nobody changes the rarity
ladder itself.** The question is only what the MESH does under it.

## THE ANSWER: KEEP THE GOLD GEOMETRY, DO NOT TINT IT TOWARD SCAR-RED.

Tinting gold toward red does not make gold look like an artifact; it makes gold
look like **copper, and then like tarnished copper**. Yellow pulled toward
(1.00, 0.13, 0.10) lands in the orange-brown band, and that band is already
doing a job in this game - it is what WEAR looks like. Mr. E's second art rule
says condition is the receipt for what a viewer paid for, and that a legendary
or artifact piece must read RICH AND NEW. A red-tinted gold reads second-hand.
We would be spending the most expensive mesh in the set to make it look used.

Nor does the ladder need the mesh's help. The hue ladder is a FRAME read - the
ring and the glow around the icon - and the mesh is a BODY read. They are in
different places in the eye, and a viewer at stream distance resolves the frame
first and the object second. A white-hot weapon inside a red artifact ring is
not a contradiction; the ring says what tier, the body says what it is.

## WHAT I WOULD DO INSTEAD, IF THE GOLD STILL FEELS WRONG IN PLACE

Put the red where red belongs on a Scar/Seam-forged piece: **in the seam, not
in the metal.** The red goes in the GLOW slot - an emissive line down the fuller,
along the weld, around the socket - and the body stays a hard, bright,
high-value metal. That gets scar-red into the silhouette at tier 5 without ever
dulling the body, it matches the forge story the names already tell, and it is
the one thing a plain working-gear voice can carry without theatre.

**AND DO NOT DECIDE THIS OFF THIS PAGE.** It is a look call and Mr. E's third
rule applies to it: build it, render it, show the picture. Two renders of the
same piece - plain `_Golden`, and `_Golden` with a red seam in the glow slot -
side by side at icon size, is five minutes of work once the meshes land, and it
settles the argument better than any paragraph. The ART lane will shoot that A/B
the run after the conversion appears, unless the game build wants it sooner.
