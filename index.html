      Welcome to Bellmire

      Bellmire is a real town, and it is being run right now, live on stream. Its heroes are the people watching: they take a name, march down the scar, win loot, and sometimes die, and the town writes every bit of it down. This page is the town’s ledger, read fresh every minute. Look at anything here; to act, you speak in Twitch chat.

                      The Bestiary

                      What Bellmire has learned of what lies below

                      A beast is marked Met once the town's remembered Chronicle names it; until then it is only a rumour, and its details stay veiled. Tells and full descriptions are not in the town's published words yet.

                      The Shops

                      What each shelf holds right now

                      Buying places an order. Your hero has to walk to that building to collect it - nothing arrives instantly, and that is not the page being broken.

                      Your Satchel

                      What you carry, what it weighs, and what to leave behind

                      The buttons type the chat command for you. They do not act instantly: your hero does it when they can, and anything that moves goods is an errand - they walk it there.

                      The Season

                      Who went deepest, earned most, died hardest and lived longest

                      Standings count the living and the fallen. A board with no data says so rather than guessing; the stream shows no leaderboard - this is where it lives.
